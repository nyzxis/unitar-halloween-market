About the Event

Programme Objectives

  • 01. Provide students with comprehensive information on bachelor's degree programmes and transfer pathways offered by UNITAR.
  • 02. Introduce students to the fundamental processes and practical procedures in planning and organising events.
  • 03. Equip students with relevant knowledge, problem-solving skills, and management practical experience.
  • 04. Provide experiential learning in entrepreneurship, retail marketing, and client handling.
