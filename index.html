About the Event

🎯 Programme Objectives

  • 01. Provide students with comprehensive information on bachelor's degree programmes and transfer pathways offered by UNITAR.
  • 02. Introduce students to the fundamental processes and practical procedures in planning and executing university events.
  • 03. Equip students with relevant knowledge, problem-solving abilities, and practical skills in management.
  • 04. Provide students with experiential learning in entrepreneurship, retail marketing, and client handling.
